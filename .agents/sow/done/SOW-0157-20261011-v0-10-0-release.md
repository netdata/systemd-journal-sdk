### Assistant Understanding

Facts:

- Root `v0.9.0` and `go/v0.9.0` exist on `netdata/systemd-journal-sdk` and peel
  to `fba9d45d53044d54163da53c903c7eedbde6e34f`; the eight Rust `0.9.0` crates
  were published from the same commit (SOW-0151). Both languages therefore
  compare against `v0.9.0`.
- Canonical `master` is `0135652d67088f806f111e76d8f5d27aa9b971ae`, 20
  non-merge commits after `v0.9.0`, including the root history retention work
  and its stable-header validation repairs.
- No `v0.10.0` or `go/v0.10.0` tag exists on the canonical repository.
- In this clone `origin` is the user's fork and `upstream` is
  `netdata/systemd-journal-sdk`.
- Compared with `v0.9.0`, Rust gains the public `Config.root_retention` field
  (`rust/src/crates/journal-log-writer/src/log/config.rs`), the
  `LogLifecycleEvent::Archived` variant
  (`rust/src/crates/journal-log-writer/src/log/mod.rs`) and the
  `WriterError::RootRetention` variant
  (`rust/src/crates/journal-log-writer/src/error.rs`). None of these
  types is `#[non_exhaustive]`. New public items: `RootRetentionFile`,
  `RootRetentionInventory`, `RootRetentionResult`, `inspect_root_retention`
  and `Log` root-retention methods.
- Go gains `LogConfig.RootRetention`, the `LogLifecycleArchived` event type,
  `RootRetentionFile`, `RootRetentionInventory`, `RootRetentionResult`,
  `InspectRootRetention` and `Log` root-retention methods. No exported Go
  symbol was removed or changed signature.

Inferences:

- `0.10.0` (minor) is required rather than `0.9.1`: the three Rust additions
  are source-breaking for exhaustive struct literals and enum matches. Under
  Cargo's pre-1.0 rules a minor bump keeps `^0.9` consumers on `0.9.x`.
  Go changes are additive; the version stays paired with Rust.

Unknowns:

- None for preparation. Publication prerequisites are recorded in SOW-0158.
